Celebration Program

The Wedding Day

08:30 AM

Guest Arrival & Auspicious Welcome

Welcoming honoured guests and family to Hotel Grand Guardian with traditional warmth, refreshing welcome drinks, and soft melodies.

09:00 AM

Morning Tea & Traditional Sweet Table

Enjoy freshly brewed Ceylon tea and coffee served alongside an authentic spread of traditional sweets and morning delicacies.

10:00 AM

Sacred Poruwa Ceremony & Blessings

Ascending the sacred Poruwa at the auspicious nekatha, tying of the nuptial cord, exchange of wedding rings, and chanting of Jayamangala Gatha.

12:30 PM

Royal Wedding Banquet & Lunch

A celebratory culinary feast featuring fine Sri Lankan dishes, international gourmet selections, and an exquisite dessert buffet.

03:30 PM

Mangala Sabhawa & Felicitations

Honouring age-old traditions with speeches of blessing, heartfelt toasts to the newlyweds, cake cutting, and congratulations from family and friends.

04:00 PM

Farewell & Joyous Departure

Sharing hugs and gratitude with all who graced the occasion as Anu and Nirmal bid farewell and embark on their journey of happily ever after.
